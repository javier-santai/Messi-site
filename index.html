Club & Country

Four chapters

Pick an era to see where he played, what he scored and what he won.

2004 – 2021

FC Barcelona

Signed as a 13-year-old after Barcelona agreed to fund his growth hormone treatment — the deal famously sketched on a paper napkin. He came through La Masia, debuted in 2004 and became the heartbeat of the greatest club side of its era under Pep Guardiola.

778Appearances
672Goals
35Trophies
  • 4 UEFA Champions League titles (2006, 2009, 2011, 2015)
  • 10 La Liga titles and 7 Copas del Rey
  • Two trebles: 2008–09 and 2014–15
  • Left in August 2021 when the club couldn't register a new contract

2021 – 2023

Paris Saint-Germain

Two seasons in Paris alongside Neymar and Kylian Mbappé. A quieter goal return than Barcelona, but a creative engine — and it was during this spell that he won the World Cup and his eighth Ballon d'Or.

75Appearances
32Goals
3Trophies
  • 2 Ligue 1 titles (2021–22, 2022–23)
  • Trophée des Champions 2022
  • Ballon d'Or 2021 and 2023 won while a PSG player

2023 – present

Inter Miami CF

Joined David Beckham's MLS club in July 2023 and transformed it almost overnight — winning the club's first-ever trophy within weeks and turning every away game into a sell-out.

2023Leagues Cup — club's first trophy
2×MLS MVP (2024, 2025)
  • Leagues Cup 2023
  • MLS Supporters' Shield 2024, with a record points tally
  • MLS Cup 2025 — the club's first league championship

2005 – present

Argentina

Years of near-misses — three straight final defeats in 2014, 2015 and 2016 — gave way to a golden run: Copa América in 2021 at the Maracanã, then the World Cup in Qatar in 2022, where he scored twice in the final and won the Golden Ball.

By the numbers

Records he holds

A handful of the marks that define his career.

Most Ballon d'Or awards

Eight, from 2009 to 2023.

Most goals in a calendar year

91 for Barcelona and Argentina in 2012, breaking Gerd Müller's 85 from 1972.

Most goals for a single club

672 for Barcelona across all competitions.

All-time La Liga top scorer

474 league goals for Barcelona.

Most European Golden Shoes

Six, the last in 2018–19.

Most World Cup appearances

26 matches across five tournaments, through Qatar 2022.

Two World Cup Golden Balls

The only player to win it twice — 2014 and 2022.

Most assists in La Liga

192 league assists for Barcelona.

Milestones

A life in moments

  1. 1987

    Born in Rosario, Argentina

    24 June, to Jorge and Celia Messi. Starts out at local club Newell's Old Boys.

  2. 2000

    Moves to Barcelona

    Joins La Masia at 13; the club covers his growth hormone treatment.

  3. 2004

    First-team debut

    Debuts for Barcelona against Espanyol aged 17.

  4. 2005

    First Barcelona goal

    Scores against Albacete, assisted by Ronaldinho. Wins the U-20 World Cup that summer.

  5. 2009

    The first treble & Ballon d'Or

    Scores a header in the Champions League final against Manchester United.

  6. 2012

    91 goals in a year

    The calendar-year record that still stands.

  7. 2021

    Copa América, then goodbye

    Ends Argentina's 28-year senior title drought, then leaves Barcelona for PSG.

  8. 2022

    World champion

    Scores twice in a 3–3 final against France, won on penalties in Lusail.

  9. 2023

    Miami & an eighth Ballon d'Or

    Joins Inter Miami and wins the Leagues Cup within a month.

  10. 2025

    MLS champion

    Leads Inter Miami to its first MLS Cup and a second straight MVP award.
